Privacy Policy

Ghata Business Ledger & Accounting

Last updated: September 2026

Ghata is designed to help users manage business accounting, customers, transactions, loans, currency exchange and related records.

Information We Process

Ghata may process account information such as email address and profile information, together with business records entered by the user.

Offline Data

Ghata supports offline use. Business records may be stored locally on the user's device so the app can continue working without internet.

Backup and Sync

When internet access is available, supported account data may be synchronized with Ghata's backend service so users can access or restore their data on supported devices.

Device Authentication

Ghata may use supported device authentication such as fingerprint, Face ID or device screen-lock authentication. Ghata does not read or store the user's device PIN, password or pattern.

Data Sharing

Ghata does not sell users' personal information. Data is processed only as necessary to provide the application's features and services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Ghata account and associated data. Visit the Delete Account page for instructions.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect account and business information. Users should also keep their devices and account credentials secure.
